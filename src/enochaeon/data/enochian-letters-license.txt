Enochian letter drawings

The letter shapes in enochian.ttf are the drawings published as individual
SVG files on Wikimedia Commons by Лорд Алекс (Xena the Rebel Girl) on
28 March 2018, under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0
Unported license.

https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/

The files are listed in Category:Enochian letters:
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Enochian_letters

This font embeds those drawings so a page can show them from disk. It is
a derivative of those drawings and stays under the same license.
