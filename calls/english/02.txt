Can the wings of the winds understand your voices of wonder
O you the second of the first
Whom the burning flames have framed within the depth of my jaws
Whom I have prepared as cups for a wedding
Or as the flowers in their beauty for the chamber of the righteous
Stronger are your feet than the barren stone
And mightier are your voices than the manifold winds
For ye are become a building such as is not
Save in the mind of the all powerful
Arise saith the first
Move therefore unto thy servants
Show yourselves in power
And make me a strong seer of things
For I am of him that liveth forever
