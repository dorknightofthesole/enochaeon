I have set my feet in the south and have looked about me
saying
Are not the thunders of increase numbered thirty three which reign in the second angle under whom I have placed nine six three nine whom none hath yet numbered but one
In whom the second beginning of things are and wax strong
Which also successively are the numbers of time
And their powers are as the first four five six
Arise ye sons of pleasure and visit the earth
For I am the Lord your God which is and liveth for ever
In the name of the creator
Move and show yourselves as pleasant deliverers that you may praise him amongst the sons of men
