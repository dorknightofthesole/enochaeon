The thunders of judgement and wrath are numbered and are harboured in the north in the likeness of an oak
Whose branches are nests of lamentation and weeping laid up for the earth
Which burn night and day
And vomit out the heads of scorpions and live sulphur mingled with poison
These be the thunders that times in the twenty fourth part of a moment roar with an hundred mighty earthquakes and a thousand times as many surges which rest not neither know any echoing time herein
One rock bringeth forth a thousand
even as the heart of man doth his thoughts
