The mighty seat groaned aloud and there were five thunders which flew into the east
And the eagle spake and cried with a loud voice
Come away
And they gathered themselves together and became the house of death of whom it is measured
They whose number is thirty one
Come away for I have prepared for you a place
Move therefore and show yourselves
Open the mysteries of your creation
Be friendly unto me
For I am the servant of the same your God
The true worshipper of the highest
